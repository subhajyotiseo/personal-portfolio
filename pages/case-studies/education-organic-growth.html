The challenge: grow beyond branded demand

The client wanted to generate as much relevant organic traffic as possible from non-branded queries. That meant competing for discovery earlier in the search journey, where users were searching by topic, need, or educational intent rather than by company name.

The strategic challenge was not simply to create more visibility. It was to build a broader and more durable search presence within the client’s niche while monitoring whether that reach translated into meaningful organic visits.

The campaign objective was to make the website discoverable when the audience searched for the subject—not only when they already knew the brand.

The SEO strategy

The campaign combined demand research, technical review, relevant third-party visibility, and continuous performance measurement.

01

Discover non-branded demand

Used Google Trends and AnswerThePublic to identify topics, questions, and changing audience interests.

02

Strengthen technical foundations

Used Screaming Frog and Search Console to identify issues affecting crawlability, visibility, and page discovery.

03

Build relevant mentions

Supported authority and discovery through guest posting, selected Web 2.0 properties, and relevant YouTube web mentions.

04

Measure and refine

Reviewed GSC and GA4 data to track visibility, acquisition patterns, and emerging optimization opportunities.

What the performance data means

The increase in impressions indicates that the website appeared for a substantially wider set of searches, supporting the campaign’s goal of building a stronger foothold in its education niche. Click growth shows that this expanded visibility also generated significantly more organic visits.

A transparent observation

CTR moved from 1.2% to 0.7% while impressions grew by more than 200%. This can happen when a website begins appearing across a much broader query set. It also identifies a clear next opportunity: improve titles, descriptions, intent alignment, and rankings for newly visible queries.

Average position improved from 13.4 to 7.8, providing additional evidence that overall search prominence strengthened during the comparison period. These figures show meaningful progress, although search performance can also be influenced by seasonality, content changes, competition, and Google updates.
