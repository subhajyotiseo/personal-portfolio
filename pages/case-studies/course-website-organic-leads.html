A confidential course-selling website needed more than website visits. It needed a steady flow of relevant organic users who could discover its courses and submit their details through an enquiry form.

Case study at a glance
  • Confidential online education and course-selling client.
  • One-year independent SEO engagement during 2025.
  • Primary goals: organic traffic and student data collection.
  • More than 250 enquiry leads generated through the website form.

The challenge: connect traffic with student demand

The website needed to attract people actively researching courses and convert that interest into usable student enquiries. Traffic alone would not be enough; content and landing pages needed to support both discovery and the next action.

The campaign therefore balanced two connected goals: broaden organic acquisition and create clearer pathways for prospective students to submit their details through the enquiry form.

The real objective was not simply more sessions. It was to turn search visibility into a dependable source of prospective student data.

  1. Researched audience demand.

The results

The GA4 report compares organic acquisition during 2025 with 2024. The chronological calculations below use 2024 as the baseline and 2025 as the campaign year.

More users acquired through organic search.

New organic users87,261 → 140,983+62%

Greater first-time discovery from search.

Organic sessions121,252 → 190,881+57%

A substantial increase in search-led visits.

Conversion outcome250+ student enquiry leads

Prospective student data was collected directly through the website enquiry form during the engagement.

What the performance data means

Organic users increased by approximately 61%, while new organic users increased by approximately 62%. This indicates that the website reached a much larger audience through unpaid search during the campaign year.

Organic sessions rose by approximately 57%, reinforcing that search became a stronger acquisition channel. Most importantly, the website generated more than 250 student enquiries, connecting the visibility work with a direct business outcome.

A transparent measurement note

The GA4 screenshot confirms acquisition growth, while the 250+ lead figure comes from website enquiry-form records. These are related outcomes, but not every organic visitor or GA4 key event should be treated as a verified student lead.
